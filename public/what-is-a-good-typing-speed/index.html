What is a good typing speed?

Words per minute is a useful score, but "good" depends on the keyboard, the language, and the kind of work you do. A careful typist with high accuracy may be more productive than a faster typist who constantly backspaces. Use Free Typing Test to measure both numbers together.

Steps

  1. Understand what WPM is counting. Most online tests convert correctly typed characters into a words-per-minute figure, often using a five-character word as the standard unit. Read the test instructions so you know whether corrections are penalized.
  2. Look at accuracy with the speed. A high WPM with a low accuracy score is usually not a good working speed. Aim to raise speed only while accuracy stays comfortable.
  3. Compare against your own work. If you type emails or code all day, compare new scores with your previous tests on the same layout, not with a single published average.
  4. Retest under similar conditions. Use the same language, keyboard, and test length. Tiredness and an unfamiliar keyboard will move the score.

There is no single passing score

Job ads sometimes mention a WPM number, but those requirements vary and are not a universal standard. If an employer gives a figure, practice toward that test format rather than toward an unofficial internet average.

Languages change the feel of a test

Accented characters in French, or a different alphabet or word length, will affect your rhythm. Choose the language you need to work in.
